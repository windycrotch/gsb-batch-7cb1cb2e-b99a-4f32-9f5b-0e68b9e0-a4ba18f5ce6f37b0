1.1.0
- The number of voices (the number of outputs) can be changed at any time during the analysis
  (before, during the processing or in the finishing stage): the notes are assigned to the voices
  independently of the limit, which only selects the voices to return, so the notes of the retained
  voices (start, end, pitch and amplitude) never change, the notes cut by the limit appear in no
  output, and re-analysing an audio with another limit gives the same result as analysing it directly
  with that limit.
- getRemainingFeatures() flushes the model output only once and does not alter the accumulated
  analysis, so reading the results again (e.g. after changing the number of voices or a parameter)
  recomputes them without duplicating the zero-padded windows.
- reset() clears the accumulated analysis before (re)loading the model, so a failed reload cannot
  carry the notes of the previous analysis into the next one.
- The model output conversion (voices and notes) is now independent of TensorFlow Lite and of the
  Vamp plugin SDK (see source/bpvp_model.h) and has a regression test (test/bpvp_convert_test.cpp,
  registered with CTest).
